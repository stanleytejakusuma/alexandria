# Phase-2 golden fact-recall measurement, v1 (2026-08-06)

First full 8-cluster measurement of the golden-synthesis load-bearing-fact
recall gate (`docs/SPEC-phase2-eval.md` >= 90%), run with the evaluator from
`WORK-ORDER-phase2-fact-recall-eval.md` after its two Red review rounds.

Method: pages generated by `scripts/synthesize-golden-pages.py` (serial, real
corpus, real LLMs via the remote unattended gateway; 70 min wall), then graded
by `scripts/eval-synthesis-fact-recall.py` with two independent model-family
graders (claude-fable-5 vs deepseek-v4-pro), strict exhaustive JSON, evidence
spans verified against the rendered page body. Cluster ids are anonymized
(cluster-1..8, golden-file order); the full report with real ids, per-fact
verdicts, evidence spans, raw grader responses, and the adjudication record
lives in the private corpus repo (`~/alexandria-corpus/.alexandria/golden/`,
`synthesis-fact-recall-v1-*.json` + `synthesis-fact-recall-v1-adjudication.jsonl`).

## The failure decomposes into two layers

1. **Writer layer (emitted pages): 18/25 = 72% consensus.** Even when the
   pipeline emits, it omits load-bearing propositions from evidence it cited
   (`evidence_gathered_but_omitted` — 3 of 4 taxonomy'd misses on graded
   clusters; 1 was `evidence_not_gathered`). Consistent with the earlier
   single-cluster readings of 3/5 and 4/5.
2. **Pipeline-emission layer: 3/8 clusters never emitted** (bounded 2-iteration
   repair loop exhausted, 12-17 min each). Their 15 facts contribute zero.

Even the friendliest bound (union recall, every dispute resolved favorably)
is 52.5% — decisively below the gate. This is a measured, attributed failure,
not an artifact: the verdict semantics (PASS / PROVISIONAL_FAIL / FINAL_FAIL /
INVALID) follow the Red-reviewed design, and the gate cannot be certified
until both layers are fixed and re-measured with the same evaluator.

## Known artifact limitation

The v1 persisted report predates a serializer fix: it retained cluster-level
fields but not the per-fact evidence spans / raw grader responses (the fix +
regression test landed 2026-08-06; future runs carry the full audit trail).
Adjudication for v1 was therefore performed against golden facts + frozen
pages directly, which is recorded in the private adjudication file.
